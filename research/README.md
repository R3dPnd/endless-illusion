One folder per engagement, e.g. `research/acme-bounty-2026-10/`.

Suggested files inside each (created by the relevant agent):

- `recon.md` — Saudade of Sunday
- `webapp.md` — Diablo of Monday
- `api.md` — Metsä of Tuesday
- `known-issues.md` — Dahlia of Wednesday
- `analysis-<cve>.md` — Sin of Friday
- `report.md` — Sen of Saturday (submission-ready)

Start each from [`../templates/engagement-report.md`](../templates/engagement-report.md).

**Do not commit** live credentials, tokens, customer data, or unredacted PII.
